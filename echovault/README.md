# Echo Vault
Time-loop puzzle. Doors open only while something stands on their matching colored plate.
Stand on a plate, press **E** to keep the loop — your echo replays it and holds the plate forever. Then walk through the door on your next loop. Level N needs N-1 echoes; use extra echoes and you earn fewer coins.

Controls: **E** keep loop · **R** discard loop · **T** reset level.
Setup: `rojo serve` in this folder, connect in Studio. All tuning in `src/shared/Config.lua`.
